About

A clinician‑educator‑researcher shaped by five health systems and one consistent standard of care.

Dr Ammar M. H. Shehadeh is a consultant paediatrician and neonatologist at Hatta Hospital, Dubai Health, and a Clinical Assistant Professor of Neonatology at Mohammed Bin Rashid University (MBRU). Over more than two decades, his practice has spanned general paediatrics and neonatal and paediatric intensive care, with particular interests in neonatal haematology and cardiology.

Since joining Hatta Hospital in 2012 — first as Senior Specialist Registrar and, from June 2025, as Consultant — he has helped build the paediatric department from the ground up: its clinical guidelines, admission criteria, audit programme, and staff training. His academic path has run in parallel, from an MSc in Neonatology with Distinction at the University of Southampton to Fellowship of the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health, and now to teaching, MMI admissions, and PhD co‑supervision at MBRU and Sri Ramachandra University, Chennai.

Trained across Damascus, Makkah, Kuwait, and London, Dr Shehadeh brings a clinician‑educator's eye to every ward round: careful bedside teaching, guideline‑driven decision‑making, and a steady commitment to evidence‑based medicine, published research, and the wellbeing of his smallest patients.
